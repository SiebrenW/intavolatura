# Literal figures with a leading-tone hint, never an automatic raise

A figuring always means exactly what the key signature and written accidentals say: an unmarked 3rd over the 5th degree in minor stays minor. Many sources omit the ♯ and expect the player to add it, but silently raising it would make the voicing staff and tablature disagree with what was written whenever the minor chord is meant. Instead the app shows a faded, bracketed (♯) hint where a raised 3rd is likely; the hint never changes the chord and can be disabled in settings.
