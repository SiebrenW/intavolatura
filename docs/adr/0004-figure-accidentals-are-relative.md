# Figure accidentals are relative to the key

A ♯, ♭ or raised (+, slashed) figure moves the note the key gives that interval up or down a semitone; it does not set a sharp or flat outright. A ♮ is absolute and gives the plain letter. This is how 17th-century sources use them: in F major a ♯ under G means B♮, and in D major a ♭ under A means C♮. Read literally those would be B♯ and C♭, which is never what was meant. Modern figures come out the same either way, so relative reading serves both.
