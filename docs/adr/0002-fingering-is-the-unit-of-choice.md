# A fingering, not a voicing, is the unit of choice

Candidates, ranking and pins are fingerings (a course and fret for every note); the voicing is derived from the fingering. On a theorbo with re-entrant tuning the same pitch can often be played on different courses (e.g. A3 on course 1 open or on course 4 at fret 2), so several fingerings share one voicing and a player chooses between them. Modelling candidates as pitch sets would hide that choice.

## Consequences

Cycling candidates can step through fingerings that look identical on the voicing staff and differ only in the tablature. That is intended.
